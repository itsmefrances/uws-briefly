Just Salad closes on Broadway. The location at 2056 Broadway, between W. 70th and W. 71st, closed Nov. 24. It opened in 2015. The company says the closure is permanent and that it was not given a reason. The sign sends you to Columbus Circle or 670 Columbus. More than 100 locations. Not this one.
