Oyishi Sushi 86 opens on W. 86th. The omakase sushi restaurant opened Nov. 20 at 70 W. 86th St., just east of Columbus. Open noon to 9:30 p.m., closed Thursdays. The space was TSS Phones and Computers, which moved to Hell's Kitchen. Used to be a phone store. Now it's omakase.
