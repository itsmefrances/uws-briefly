Lemongrass Lounge opens on Columbus. The Vietnamese restaurant opened Nov. 13 at 1000 Columbus Ave., between W. 109th and W. 110th. Pho, banh mi, noodle soups and rice bowls, noon to 10 p.m. daily. It replaces Red Hot, the hot pot place. Students get 10% off. Everyone else pays full price.
