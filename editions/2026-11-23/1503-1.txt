Central Park parking lot to become a soccer field. The lot next to Tavern on the Green, near W. 67th and Central Park West, gets a permanent mini-pitch open to the public. The state is putting in $5.5 million; the Conservancy and the city add $500,000 each. Gov. Hochul announced it Monday. Robert Moses paved it for the restaurant. It's usually empty.
