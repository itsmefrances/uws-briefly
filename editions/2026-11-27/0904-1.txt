UWS Citi Bike docks keep running empty. A West Side Rag analysis of public data found UWS docks completely empty about 21% of the time between 9 a.m. and noon. Morning arrivals replaced 40% of the bikes taken out. On the Upper East Side, 77%. Lyft, which runs Citi Bike, cites hilly terrain and the Hudson. The East Side has hills and a river too.
