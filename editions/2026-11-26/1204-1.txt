Police: Riverside Park stabbing story was made up. The NYPD says the report that a stranger stabbed a 26-year-old woman Monday night was fabricated. Police say she then told them she stabbed herself by accident, and that video shows her buying the knife beforehand. They are no longer looking for a suspect. This updates our Nov. 24 post.
